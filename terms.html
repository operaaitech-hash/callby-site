CALLBY arranges for a vetted professional chauffeur to come to your car and drive you, in your car, to your destination (CALLBY Now and CALLBY Reserve) or for a booked number of hours (CALLBY Private). The chauffeur reaches your car by their own means and does not bring a vehicle. CALLBY operates within the service area shown in the app.
